What Colleagues Say

A 150-word summary distilled from years of recognition and feedback from people I have worked with at JLR over the last 10 years.

Across a decade at JLR, colleagues consistently describe Andrew as a trusted, dependable and inventive problem solver who combines deep technical expertise with a strong commitment to people.

Recognition repeatedly highlights his integrity, patience, curiosity and willingness to help, whether resolving urgent issues, supporting colleagues, explaining complex changes or stepping beyond his normal remit.

From SuccessFactors, C4S and SharePoint to Power Platform applications, automation, UAT and critical operational fixes, Andrew is recognised for delivering at pace without losing sight of quality or user experience. Colleagues also praise his collaboration, humour, clear communication and readiness to support teams during go-lives, outages and tight deadlines. The recurring message is simple: Andrew makes complicated problems manageable, delivers when it matters and helps people succeed together.
